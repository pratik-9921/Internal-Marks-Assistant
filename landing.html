Features

Nothing you don't.

Enter CIE, MSE, SEE, TW, PR, OR marks per course. Instant percentage calculation with max-mark validation. Blank inputs stay Pending — never forced to 0.

Student Dashboard with Charts
Bar charts for subject performance, doughnut for assessment breakdown, radial progress for 650 marks — all live.

Faculty Roster with Search & Filter

How it works

Up and running in minutes.

1
Enter your marks

Open the Marks Calculator, select your elective tracks (PEC-I, MDM-III, OE-II), and type in your CIE, MSE, TW, PR and OR marks per course. Live validation catches errors instantly.

2
See your standing

The Dashboard shows your total out of 650, percentage of evaluated marks, semester standing, credits covered, and a chart of all 9 courses — updated in real-time.

3
Ask the AI

Type any question — "Which subjects do I need to focus on?" or "Who is the class top performer?" — and Gemini 2.5 Flash calls the right tool and gives you a grounded answer.

📋 Demo Prototype — Read This

This is a client-side demonstration. All student names, roll numbers, and marks are entirely fictional and stored in browser localStorage only. Do not enter real student data.

A production deployment would require server-side authentication, role-based access control, an immutable audit log, and a real database — none of which are present here. The Gemini AI assistant may make errors; always verify marks against official records.
